# Initial four-business ideation pass

Generated September 11, 2026 from the saved product briefs and owner direction. These are proposals, not researched evidence, selected work, or authorized external actions. The matching in-product seed is in `scripts/seed-ideation.mjs`; each proposal can be saved and edited in Explore before moving to Do.

## AlignIQ Golf

1. **Coach-led first-round cohort** — Recruit a very small owner-approved cohort through golf coaches, then observe whether eligible golfers complete a first logged round and return to review it.
2. **One-shot reflection content series** — Develop a repeatable short-form series showing how one round observation becomes one focused practice decision, without making performance promises.
3. **Golf instructor distribution shortlist** — Research instructors and communities whose public teaching emphasizes reflection, practice planning, or on-course decision-making.

## Astro-Log

1. **Seven-morning reflection ritual** — Create a seven-part morning sequence demonstrating the habit around daily briefs while avoiding scientific or guaranteed-outcome claims.
2. **Astrology creator collaboration shortlist** — Research creators whose public work fits thoughtful daily astrology and transparent entertainment positioning.
3. **Email-to-first-value friction brief** — Inspect signup through the first useful morning brief and document where readiness, comprehension, or delivery may break.

## Tonight

1. **Ten-pair dinner decision pilot** — Recruit a small local cohort to use shared dinner shortlists and record whether the product helps them reach an actual decision.
2. **Local food creator decision challenge** — Develop a creator concept built around a constrained shortlist and shared choice without implying a restaurant partnership.
3. **Share-to-final-choice improvement brief** — Review invitation through final choice and specify the smallest change most likely to reduce indecision.

## Traction OS

1. **Four-business proof narrative** — Document how Explore, Do, evidence, and portfolio priority change real owner decisions, separating artifacts from outcomes.
2. **Founder-operator guided pilot** — Define a small pilot for founders managing several products, with a reviewed pitch and explicit first-use observation before any sending.
3. **Evidence-to-next-action product brief** — Improve the surface distinguishing drafted work, executed actions, observed results, and the next owner decision.

## Suggested first choices

- AlignIQ Golf: coach-led first-round cohort.
- Astro-Log: email-to-first-value friction brief before scaling creator distribution.
- Tonight: ten-pair dinner decision pilot.
- Traction OS: four-business proof narrative using actual evidence created by the other three pilots.

This sequence creates a useful holding-company loop: Traction OS helps operate the three product pilots, then uses its own recorded decisions and outcomes as product proof.
